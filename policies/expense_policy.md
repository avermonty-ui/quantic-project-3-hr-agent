# Business Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses may be reimbursed and how employees should submit reimbursement requests. Northstar People Operations reimburses reasonable, necessary, and properly documented expenses that support approved business activities.

## General Requirements

To qualify for reimbursement, an expense must be business-related, reasonable in amount, approved when required, supported by a receipt, and submitted within thirty days of purchase.

Employees should use company-approved vendors, tools, and booking processes when available.

## Manager Approval

Expenses over $250 require manager approval before reimbursement. Expenses over $1,000 require both manager and Finance approval unless covered by a pre-approved budget.

Unapproved expenses may be denied even if they are business-related.

## Home Office Equipment

Remote-eligible employees may request reimbursement for basic home office equipment when the equipment is necessary for their role. Examples include a keyboard, mouse, monitor, webcam, headset, or ergonomic accessories.

Chairs, desks, and other furniture require manager and HR approval before purchase. Reimbursement may be denied if the item was not pre-approved.

## Computers and Company Devices

Employees should not purchase laptops, tablets, phones, or other computing devices for reimbursement unless IT has approved the purchase in advance.

Company devices must be configured by IT and must meet company security standards.

## Travel Expenses

Approved business travel expenses may include airfare, lodging, ground transportation, parking, meals, and required business communications. Employees should choose reasonable, cost-effective options.

Personal upgrades, entertainment, travel for companions, and non-business side trips are not reimbursable.

## Meals

Meals during approved business travel or approved customer meetings may be reimbursed if reasonable and supported by receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless explicitly approved for a business event by an executive.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are generally not reimbursable:

- Personal entertainment
- Commuting from home to the normal work location
- Traffic or parking fines
- Personal clothing
- Unapproved software subscriptions
- Personal device purchases
- Luxury upgrades
- Expenses without receipts

## Submission Process

Employees should submit reimbursement requests through the HR or Finance system. Each request should include the employee ID, business purpose, date, amount, vendor, receipt, and required approvals.

## Exceptions

Exceptions require written approval from the employee's manager and Finance. HR may review exceptions involving remote work, accommodations, or employee relations.
