# Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains when employees may work remotely and what approvals are required. Northstar People Operations supports flexible work when it is compatible with job duties, team needs, security requirements, and legal obligations.

## Eligibility

Remote work eligibility depends on the employee's role, department, performance expectations, location, and access to company systems. Employees marked as remote eligible in HR records may request temporary or recurring remote work.

Some roles require on-site work because they involve physical equipment, in-person operations, facilities support, or direct customer interaction. Employees in roles that are not remote eligible may not work remotely unless HR approves an exception.

## Short-Term Remote Work

Employees may request short-term remote work for personal appointments, focused work, temporary transportation issues, or short-term family needs. Short-term remote work within the employee's current state or country may be approved by the manager if business needs are met.

## Extended Remote Work

Remote work lasting more than ten consecutive business days requires manager and HR approval. HR must review the request for payroll, tax, labor law, benefits, data security, and insurance implications.

Employees may not begin extended remote work until approval is documented.

## Out-of-State or International Remote Work

Employees who want to work from another state or country must submit the request before travel begins. HR must review all out-of-state or international remote work requests.

International remote work is not automatically approved. It may create immigration, tax, payroll, cybersecurity, data privacy, and employment law obligations. Employees should not assume they may work internationally even if their role is remote eligible.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must use company-approved devices, approved collaboration tools, strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, and secure internet connections. Employees may not use public computers or shared personal devices to access company systems.

Employees must immediately report lost devices, suspected phishing, unauthorized access, or accidental disclosure of company information.

## Work Hours and Availability

Remote employees must remain available during agreed working hours. They are expected to attend meetings, respond to business communications, and maintain performance standards.

Managers may require remote employees to attend in-person meetings, training, customer events, or team activities when business needs require.

## Denial or Revocation

Remote work may be denied or revoked if the arrangement affects performance, team coverage, customer support, security, compliance, or business operations.

## Required Documentation

Approved remote work arrangements should document the employee name, employee ID, remote location, expected dates, manager approval, HR approval if required, and any security conditions.
