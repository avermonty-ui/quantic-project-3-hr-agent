# Paid Time Off Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how employees of Northstar People Operations may request and use paid time off, sick leave, and floating holidays. The goal is to help employees plan time away from work while ensuring teams have enough coverage.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees are eligible for paid time off beginning on their first day of employment. Part-time employees may receive prorated paid time off if specified in their employment agreement. Contractors and temporary workers are not eligible for company paid time off unless required by law or contract.

## PTO Balances

Employees may view their available PTO balance in the HR system. PTO balances are tracked in days. Employees are responsible for checking their available balance before submitting a PTO request.

If an employee does not have enough PTO available for a requested absence, the request may be denied, shortened, or converted to unpaid leave with manager and HR approval.

## Request Requirements

Employees should submit PTO requests at least five business days before the planned absence when possible. Requests for three or more consecutive workdays require manager approval.

Requests during critical business periods, product launches, quarterly close, or scheduled customer events may require additional review.

## Approval Process

The employee's direct manager reviews PTO requests based on the employee's available balance, team coverage, project deadlines, and business needs. HR may be consulted if the request involves leave law, disability accommodation, extended absence, or unpaid leave.

A PTO request is not approved until the manager confirms approval in writing or through the HR system.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave may be used for an employee's illness, medical appointments, recovery, or care for an immediate family member. Employees should notify their manager as soon as practical if they need to use sick leave unexpectedly.

Managers should not ask employees to disclose detailed medical information. HR should be contacted if the absence may qualify for protected leave or accommodation.

## Floating Holidays

Eligible employees may use floating holidays for personal, cultural, religious, or other observances. Floating holidays should be requested in advance when possible.

Unused floating holidays do not carry over unless required by local law or approved by HR.

## Extended Absences

Employees requesting more than ten consecutive workdays away should contact HR before submitting the request. HR will determine whether the request should be handled as PTO, unpaid leave, protected leave, or another leave category.

## Policy Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy require approval from both the employee's manager and HR. Exceptions should be documented in the HR system.
