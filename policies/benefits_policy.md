# Employee Benefits Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes eligibility and general rules for employee benefits at Northstar People Operations. Benefits are intended to support employee health, wellbeing, and financial security.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees are generally eligible for company benefits. Part-time employees, contractors, interns, and temporary workers may not be eligible unless required by law or stated in a written agreement.

Eligibility is determined by employment type, scheduled hours, work location, and applicable law.

## Enrollment

Eligible employees may enroll in benefits during onboarding, annual open enrollment, or after a qualifying life event. Qualifying life events may include marriage, divorce, birth or adoption of a child, loss of other coverage, or a change in employment status.

Employees must submit enrollment changes within the required enrollment window. Late requests may need to wait until the next open enrollment period unless required by law.

## Medical, Dental, and Vision Coverage

Eligible employees may choose from available medical, dental, and vision plans. Plan options may vary by location and employment classification.

Employees are responsible for reviewing plan details, coverage levels, payroll deductions, provider networks, and dependent eligibility before enrolling.

## Dependent Coverage

Eligible employees may enroll qualified dependents if the plan allows dependent coverage. Employees may be required to provide documentation proving dependent eligibility.

## Benefits for Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees are not automatically eligible for full benefits. HR will determine whether a part-time employee qualifies for any benefits based on scheduled hours, local law, and company policy.

## Changes in Employment Status

A change from full-time to part-time status may affect benefits eligibility. A change from part-time to full-time status may create benefits eligibility beginning on the effective date determined by HR.

Employees should contact HR before a planned status change if they have questions about benefits impact.

## Leave and Benefits

Employees on approved leave may be able to continue certain benefits depending on the leave type, duration, applicable law, and plan rules. HR will provide guidance for extended leave situations.

## Questions and Escalation

Employees with benefits questions should contact HR. If the employee's eligibility status is unclear, HR should review the employee profile, employment type, location, and benefits record before providing a final answer.
